## Waiting period rules (IRDAI standard)

| Category | Waiting period | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Initial (all policies) | 1 month | Everything except accidents |
| Specific diseases | 24 months | Gallstones (K80), Hernia (K40/K41), Cataract (H26), Appendicitis (K35), Disc disorders (M51) |
| Pre-existing diseases | 48 months | Any condition declared or reasonably known at policy start |
| Accidents | 0 months | Trauma, fractures from accidents — no waiting period |
| Maternity | Varies (9–24 months) | O80, O82 — check specific policy terms |
